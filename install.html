  4. 4

    Tap "Add" in the top-right corner.

  5. 5

    Find the Hill Slayer icon on your home screen and tap it. That's your app! 🎉

One thing — do this first!

The very first time you open it, be on WiFi or cell signal. That lets it save itself for offline use. After that, it works anywhere — even deep on the trail with zero bars.

Once you're in

  • ✓Tap any Saturday to see that week's distance, pace, location, and stretches.
  • ✓Check off each run as you finish — your progress saves automatically.
  • ✓Tap Cap10K Elevation Guide to study the hills before race day.
  • ✓Slide the pace & heart-rate sliders to match how you feel — every target updates.
